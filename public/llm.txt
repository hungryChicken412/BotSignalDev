# BotSignal.Dev

> Scan your website for AI discoverability and see if LLM bots can find and understand your website. Fix LLM compatibility and SEO issues in under 60 seconds.

## About Us
BotSignal.Dev provides automated website auditing, scraping, and AI-readiness scoring to evaluate whether your site can be crawled and understood by Large Language Models (LLMs) like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity.
