# The Ultimate Guide to BotSignal: Audit Your Site for AI in 60 Seconds

The search landscape has shifted. If your website isn't optimized for Large Language Models (LLMs) like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity, you are effectively invisible to the next generation of internet users. 

But how do you actually *know* if an AI can read your site? 

That's exactly why we built **BotSignal.Dev**. In this guide, we will walk you through how to run your first AI compatibility audit, interpret your scores, and apply the fixes necessary to become highly discoverable.

<Callout type="info">
  **Quick Note:** You don't need to write a single line of code to run a scan. BotSignal handles the heavy lifting of simulating AI crawler behavior and semantic extraction in the background.
</Callout>

## Step 1: Run Your First Audit

Getting started is entirely frictionless. You don't need a credit card, and you don't even need to create an account for your first basic scan.

1. Navigate to the **BotSignal.Dev Homepage**.
2. Locate the **MVP Live** scanner section.
3. Drop in your website URL (e.g., `https://yourstartup.com`).
4. Sign in securely with Google to verify your request and save your history.
5. Click **Audit Site**.

Behind the scenes, our engines instantly scrape your visible text, extract semantic SEO tags, and evaluate your DOM structure exactly how an LLM bot would.

## Step 2: Understand Your AI Readiness Score

Once the scan is complete, you will be taken to your **Overview Dashboard**. The most important metric here is your **AI Readiness Score** (scored from 0 to 100).

*   **90 - 100 (Excellent):** Your site is highly structured, rich in factual data, and easily digestible by AI. 
*   **70 - 89 (Warning):** AI can read your site, but it might struggle to extract key entities or get confused by missing schema markup.
*   **0 - 69 (Critical):** Your site is actively blocking AI crawlers, or the content is completely obscured by JavaScript or marketing fluff.

<Callout type="warning">
  **Important:** Don't panic if your first score is low! Most modern websites are built for human eyes and traditional Google crawlers, not AI agents. Fixing this is easier than you think.
</Callout>

## Step 3: Dive Into the Four Core Modules

Your dashboard breaks down your AI compatibility into four distinct dimensions.

### 1. AI Discovery & Crawler Access
Can bots actually reach your content? We check your core files (`robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, and the emerging `llms.txt` standard). We also test your firewall access against major AI user agents like `GPTBot`, `ClaudeBot`, and `PerplexityBot`.

### 2. Information Density
LLMs hate fluff; they want raw, extractable facts. This module calculates the ratio of hard data to marketing jargon. If your page is full of "synergistic paradigms" but lacks pricing or clear features, your density score will plummet.

### 3. Citation Potential
Are you quote-worthy? We scan your text for clear definitions, statistical claims, FAQ structures, and data tables. We even show you *Predicted AI Citations*—exactly how an LLM would likely quote your content in a generated response.

### 4. Technical SEO & Schema
Traditional SEO still matters for AI. We audit your DOM structure, broken links, redirect chains, and most importantly, your **JSON-LD Schema Markup**. Proper schema tells the AI exactly what your business does without it having to guess.

## Step 4: Execute the Action Plan

Knowing your score is only half the battle. BotSignal provides a prioritized **Action Plan** to help you fix your issues efficiently.

Navigate to the **AI Insights** tab on your dashboard. Here you will find:
*   **Quick Wins:** High-impact fixes categorized by time (e.g., 2-minute fixes vs 4-minute fixes) and points gained.
*   **Strengths & Weaknesses:** Exactly what an AI *can* answer about your site, and what it *cannot*. 
*   **Content Strategy:** Recommendations for new article types or FAQ tactics to capture more AI real estate.
