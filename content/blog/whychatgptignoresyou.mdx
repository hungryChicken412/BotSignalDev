# Why ChatGPT Doesn't Know Your Business Exists (And How to Fix It)

Imagine this: A potential customer opens up ChatGPT, Claude, or Perplexity and types, *"What's the best local coffee shop for working remotely?"* or *"What software should I use to manage my freelance invoices?"*

The AI thinks for a second, types out a beautifully formatted answer, and... recommends your biggest competitor. 

Ouch. 

You have a great website. You spent weeks on the design. You even paid for SEO optimization so you rank high on Google. So why is the AI acting like you don't exist?

The answer is simple: **AI assistants don't read the internet the same way humans (or Google) do.**

<Callout type="info">
  **The Big Shift:** Google looks for *keywords* and *links* to rank your website in a list. AI assistants look for *facts* and *clear answers* to synthesize a conversation. 
</Callout>

Here are three simple, non-technical reasons why AI might be ignoring your website—and how to fix them today.

## 1. You're Using Too Much "Marketing Fluff"

Humans love a good story, but AI just wants the facts. 

If your website's homepage says something like, *"We empower synergistic paradigms to elevate your daily workflow,"* an AI has absolutely no idea what you actually sell. Is it software? Is it a consulting firm? Is it coffee?

**The Fix:** Be painfully clear. 
Somewhere on your homepage, you need a sentence that a five-year-old (or a robot) can understand. *"We sell simple invoicing software for freelance graphic designers."* AI engines love high "information density"—which is just a fancy way of saying "getting straight to the point."

## 2. You Make It Hard to Find the Basics

Think about the questions your customers ask you every single day. 
* *How much does it cost?*
* *Do you offer free shipping?*
* *Does this integrate with Shopify?*

If an AI can't find the answers to these questions in a few seconds, it won't recommend you. It will move on to a competitor who makes this information obvious.

**The Fix:** Create a killer FAQ page. 
AI assistants absolutely love Frequently Asked Questions. By formatting your content as a direct Question and Answer, you are practically spoon-feeding the AI exactly what it needs to recommend you.

<Callout type="warning">
  **Quick Tip:** Don't hide your pricing! If an AI is asked for "affordable CRM tools" and you hide your prices behind a "Contact for Quote" button, the AI will likely skip you entirely.
</Callout>

## 3. Your Website is All Style, No Substance

We all love a beautiful website with sliding animations, cool videos, and text that magically appears as you scroll. But AI assistants are essentially blind. They can't "watch" your explainer video, and they often get confused by complex animations.

If all the important information about your business is locked inside an image, a video, or an interactive graphic, the AI won't see it.

**The Fix:** Write it out.
Always ensure that your core offerings, features, and benefits are written in plain, simple text. If you have a great explainer video, include a brief text summary right below it. 

## The Bottom Line

You don't need to be a software engineer to get noticed by AI. You just need to be clear, direct, and helpful.
