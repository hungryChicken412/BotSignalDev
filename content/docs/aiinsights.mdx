# AI Insights & Strategy

While technical SEO ensures your site can be crawled, the **AI Insights** module evaluates whether your site can be *understood*. 

Rather than relying on static heuristic checks, this module feeds your parsed, visible website text directly into a Large Language Model (LLM). This simulates exactly how generative agents—like ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Claude—synthesize your business offering and highlights the exact gaps preventing them from recommending your product.

---

## Executive Summary

The top of your Insights dashboard provides a direct look into the "mind" of the AI. 

*   **AI Synthesis:** We ask the model to generate a concise, one-paragraph description of your business using *only* the text scraped from your provided URL. If this summary sounds inaccurate or misses your core value proposition, it indicates that real-world AI agents will also misunderstand your product.
*   **Action Priority:** A high-level directive (`Low`, `Medium`, or `High`) indicating the urgency of your content gaps. A `High` priority means the AI fundamentally failed to extract critical business entities.

---

## AI Comprehension Analysis

To provide actionable feedback, the model generates realistic user queries and attempts to answer them using your content. The results are split into two diagnostic categories:

### What AI Understands
This section highlights the user questions your website successfully answered. More importantly, it provides technical context on **Why it succeeded**. 

For example, the AI might note that it easily extracted your pricing model because your data was housed within a semantic `<table>` rather than buried in a narrative paragraph.

### AI Blindspots
This section exposes critical questions a user might ask an AI search engine that your website fails to answer. Each blindspot includes:

*   **The Missing Query:** The exact question the AI could not resolve.
*   **Why It Matters:** The technical or structural reason the AI failed (e.g., low information density, missing schema).
*   **The Fix:** A highly specific, actionable recommendation to resolve the gap.
*   **Business Impact:** A concise assessment of what this blindspot costs you, such as a "Missed Citation Opportunity" or "Poor Technical Discoverability".

## Growth Action Plan

Based on your semantic strengths and blindspots, the system generates a tailored, multi-tiered content strategy.

### Quick Wins
A targeted list of immediate, low-effort adjustments you can make to your existing copy. These typically include extracting key statistics into bulleted lists, adding TL;DR summaries to long-form articles, or standardizing terminology across your site.

### Content Strategy Focus
Long-term recommendations for new content creation. The LLM identifies the specific **Article Types Needed** to improve your visibility in generative search results. 

> Rather than generic blog posts, the system may recommend building "Definitional Hubs," "Comparison Matrices," or "Step-by-step Procedures" based on what data the AI is actively seeking.

### FAQ Structure Tactics
Guidelines on how to restructure your Frequently Asked Questions. AI models rely heavily on deep, structured coverage of specific concepts. This section advises on how to frame your headings and questions to exactly match the prompts users are feeding into LLMs.
